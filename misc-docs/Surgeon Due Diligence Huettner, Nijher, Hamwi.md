## How to read this

Each finding carries a severity and an evidence label. Severity is how much it should weigh on your decision if true; evidence is how sure we can be that it is true. Scope is professional conduct only; personal matters are left out.

| Severity | What qualifies |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | License discipline or restriction, practice-related criminal charges, a patient death tied to the surgeon, loss of hospital privileges |
| High | Malpractice claims alleging serious injury or ending in a payment, regulatory action against the office or surgical facility, repeated credible reports of the same serious complication |
| Medium | Malpractice claims over minor injury closed without payment, lawsuits not about patient injury (billing, contracts, employment), formal complaints such as BBB, a single credible report of a serious complication, misleading advertising |
| Low | Service problems: wait times, staff, communication, surprise costs, results the patient disliked without a complication |

| Evidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Official record | Court docket, state license file, board or agency record |
| Credible report | Named news outlet, or a detailed review by an identifiable patient |
| Unverified | Anonymous post, rumor, or a claim not tied to any record; kept low weight |

## Before you book

These checks take a few minutes and cover what my searches couldn't reach; the consult questions target each surgeon's open issues.

Checks to run yourself:

- [ ] Confirm each surgeon's board certification on the [American Board of Plastic Surgery](https://www.abplasticsurgery.org/) site; its lookup needs a CAPTCHA, which blocked me.
- [ ] Look up Dr. Nijher's two Marion County cases (19-CA-1611 and 24-CA-001589 AX) on the Marion County Clerk's site to see what was alleged.
- [ ] Search Sarasota County court records for Dr. Hamwi and Sumter County records for Dr. Huettner. Pending lawsuits show up only there; the state database lists claims only after they close.
- [ ] Recheck all three licenses on the [Florida DOH lookup](https://mqa-internet.doh.state.fl.us/MQASearchServices/Home) the week you book.

Ask every surgeon:

- Will you personally perform the whole operation, and where exactly?
- What anesthesia will I have, and who gives and monitors it?
- Can I see before-and-after photos of massive-weight-loss patients for this exact procedure, and how many do you do a year?
- What will a revision cost me? Please put deposit, refund and cancellation terms in writing.

Surgeon-specific questions:

- **Huettner:** Your website describes an accredited office surgical center. Will my surgery be at TLC, the Lake County center or a hospital? Do you still practice in New York, and given that and your AdventHealth role, who handles my follow-up and any complications?
- **Nijher:** Have you had malpractice claims, and what did you change afterward? A candid answer should match the record: three, one paid. If insurance covers part of my surgery, what happens under the 60-day clause if my insurer is slow?
- **Hamwi:** Would my arm lift be awake, under IV sedation or under general anesthesia, and where? If IV, who administers it, and is that facility accredited? Is the $1,500 deposit refunded if you decline me after pre-op testing? Who handles wound problems once I'm home, roughly two and a half hours away? Can you show both board certificates?
