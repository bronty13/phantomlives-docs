# Slime Bar Business Plan

A DIY slime cart for kids' events. Kids pick their base, mix-ins, and charms, like a charcuterie cart. A cookie cart could come later using the same breakdown cart (DIY cart from Etsy, about $350).

## Make vs. Buy

**Make it yourself, but only two bases:**

- **Clear base:** for watery, glassy, and crunchy styles
- **White/glossy base:** for cloud and butter styles

All customizing happens at the cart, so the base never changes, only the toppings do. Making it is much cheaper per ounce than buying pre-made, and you control the texture. Buying pre-made in bulk is fine for the first event or two while you practice.

## Textures

| Texture | How to make it |
|---|---|
| **Watery (glassy, clear)** | Clear glue with a little less activator, so it stays loose and stretchy. Make it 3–5 days ahead; it looks cloudy at first and clears up sitting sealed. |
| **Cloud (soft, fluffy)** | White slime plus expanded instant snow powder. Mix fresh at the cart, because it dries out faster. |
| **Floam / crunchy** | Clear slime plus foam beads or fishbowl beads |
| **Butter** | White slime plus soft air-dry clay |
| **Glitter, color, scent** | Drops and shakers at each station |

## Seasonal Themes

Themes are just a topping swap.

- **Fall festival:** orange and brown color, pumpkin spice scent, leaf and pumpkin charms
- **Other options:** Christmas, back-to-school, birthdays, using the same approach

**Charm sources:** Amazon, Etsy wholesale shops, Oriental Trading, AliExpress (order early; it's slow).

## Shelf Life

- Store in airtight tubs and handle with clean hands and scoops.
- Homemade glue slime typically lasts weeks, sometimes longer. It spoils when water or dirty hands get in.
- Make bases a few days before each event. Don't pre-mix add-ins into stored stock.

## Steps, In Order

1. **Register the business:** business license and sales tax registration with the state.
2. **Get insurance through Kirsten:** general liability for events, and whether a DIY kids' slime bar raises product-safety (CPSIA) questions.
3. **Order test supplies and practice at home:** nail down recipes and per-cup cost.
4. **Price it and do one small event,** then buy in bulk based on what sold.

## First Supply Order

- [ ] **Glue:** 1 gallon clear PVA and 1 gallon white PVA (each makes roughly 25–30 small cups)
- [ ] **Activator:** borax, or contact solution plus baking soda (borax-free option)
- [ ] **Instant snow powder** for cloud slime
- [ ] **Foam beads and fishbowl beads**
- [ ] **Colorants:** gel color or liquid watercolor
- [ ] **Scents:** skin-safe fragrance oils
- [ ] **Glitter:** a few shaker jars
- [ ] **Charms:** a mixed assortment plus one seasonal theme
- [ ] **Cups:** 4 oz deli cups with lids, a few hundred
- [ ] **Station supplies:** scoops, spatulas, mixing bowls
- [ ] **Labels:** ingredients and logo
- [ ] **Cleanup:** gloves, wipes, hand sanitizer, table covers
